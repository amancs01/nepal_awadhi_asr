# Research Scope

STATUS: PHASE 0 — RESEARCH VALIDATION

## Working Title

Cross-Border Robustness and Contact-Language Transfer for Low-Resource Awadhi Speech Recognition in Nepal

## Scope Statement

This repository supports scientific validation of the research premise before data collection, model training, or novelty claims. The proposal is an initial hypothesis and may change substantially after evidence is reviewed.

## In Scope During Phase 0

- Validate prior work on Awadhi ASR and related Indic low-resource ASR.
- Audit existing datasets, especially speaker geography, variety labels, speech style, licensing, and transcription conventions.
- Inventory candidate ASR models and identify whether they are practical baselines or controlled research models.
- Refine research questions without freezing them prematurely.
- Identify confounds that would invalidate cross-border, donor-language, or language-fidelity claims.
- Document ethics risks and future approval needs.

## Out of Scope During Phase 0

- Nepal-Awadhi speech collection.
- Downloading large datasets.
- Training or fine-tuning ASR models.
- GPU-heavy experiments.
- Final paper conclusions.
- Claims of novelty or first corpus status.
- Speculative ML training code.
