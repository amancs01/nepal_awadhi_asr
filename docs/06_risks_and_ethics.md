# Risks And Ethics

STATUS: PHASE 0 — RESEARCH VALIDATION

This document records ethical, social, legal, and methodological risks.

## Initial Risk Areas

- UNKNOWN: Consent requirements for any future speech collection.
- UNKNOWN: Licensing status of candidate datasets.
- UNKNOWN: Community preferences for language naming and dialect labeling.
- UNKNOWN: Risks from cross-border linguistic framing.
- UNKNOWN: Potential harms from inaccurate ASR outputs.
- UNKNOWN: Risks of overclaiming representation or novelty.

## Phase 0 Safeguards

- Do not collect speech data.
- Do not download large datasets.
- Do not train models.
- Record uncertainty.
- Treat negative findings as valid research outcomes.
- Avoid claiming representation of a community before scope and consent questions are addressed.
- Avoid storing unnecessary personal identifying information in future metadata.
